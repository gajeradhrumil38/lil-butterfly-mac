three easy steps

Install Butterfly in a minute.

  1. 01
    Download

    Open the latest release and click the single Butterfly ZIP under Assets.

  2. 02
    Move to Applications

    Unzip it, then drag Butterfly.app into your Applications folder.

  3. 03
    Open Butterfly

    Look for the butterfly in your menu bar, then choose “Show a butterfly now”.

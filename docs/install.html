simple Mac installation

Install Butterfly.

There is only one download. It works on both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 13 or newer.

Download Butterfly ↗
1

Download the ZIP

Under Assets, click the only file that starts with Butterfly and ends in .zip, for example:

Butterfly-1.0.0.zip

Ignore the two “Source code” downloads.

2

Move it to Applications

Open Downloads, double-click the ZIP, then drag Butterfly.app into your Applications folder.

3

Open Butterfly

In Applications, Control-click or right-click Butterfly and choose Open. Confirm Open once more.

If macOS still blocks it: open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, then choose Open Anyway.

4

Look in the menu bar

Butterfly stays out of the Dock. Find the 🦋 icon at the top of the screen, click it, and choose Show a butterfly now.

Updating is just as simple

When you see 🦋↑, click it and choose Update available. Download the new ZIP and replace Butterfly in Applications. Your preferences are kept.

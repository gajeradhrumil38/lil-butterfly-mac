# Install Butterfly

You only need to copy and paste one command.

## 1. Open Terminal

Press **⌘ Command + Space**, type **Terminal**, then press **Return**.

## 2. Copy and paste this command

```bash
/bin/bash -c "$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gajeradhrumil38/lil-butterfly-mac/main/scripts/install.sh)"
```

Press **Return**.

> Your Mac may ask for your password. Type it and press Return. Nothing appears while you type your password—that is normal.

## 3. Find the butterfly

Butterfly opens automatically.

Look for **🦋 at the top of your screen**, click it, and choose **Show a butterfly now**.

## Updates

When the icon changes to **🦋↑**:

1. Click the icon.
2. Choose **Update available**.
3. Run the same installation command above.

The newest Butterfly will be installed and your preferences will stay saved.

## Quit it by accident?

Quitting Butterfly doesn't uninstall it — it's still in your **Applications** folder. To open it again:

- Press **⌘ Command + Space**, type **Butterfly**, then press Return, or
- Open it from your **Applications** folder.

## Need help?

- Look for 🦋 in the menu bar at the top of the screen.
- Make sure Butterfly is not paused.

You can also open the separate [installation page](INSTALL.md).

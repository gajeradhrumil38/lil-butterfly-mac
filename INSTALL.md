# Install Butterfly on Mac

You do not need Terminal or Xcode. The download works on both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 13 or newer.

## 1. Download

Open the [latest Butterfly release](https://github.com/gajeradhrumil38/lil-butterfly-mac/releases/latest).

Under **Assets**, click the only file that starts with **Butterfly** and ends in **.zip**, for example:

`Butterfly-1.0.0.zip`

Do not download the “Source code” files.

## 2. Put Butterfly in Applications

1. Open your **Downloads** folder.
2. Double-click the ZIP file.
3. Drag **Butterfly.app** into the **Applications** folder.

## 3. Open it for the first time

Because the app is not yet notarized, double-clicking may show a macOS warning.

1. Open **Applications** in Finder.
2. Control-click or right-click **Butterfly**.
3. Choose **Open**.
4. Choose **Open** again in the confirmation window.

If macOS still blocks it, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click **Open Anyway** beside Butterfly.

## 4. Find Butterfly

Butterfly does not appear in the Dock. Look for the **🦋 icon in the menu bar** at the top of the screen.

Click it and choose **Show a butterfly now**.

## Updates

When an update exists, the menu-bar icon changes to **🦋↑**.

1. Click **🦋↑**.
2. Choose **Update available**.
3. Download the new Butterfly ZIP and replace the old Butterfly app in Applications.

Your preferences remain saved in:

`~/Library/Application Support/Butterfly/config.json`

## Optional: open at login

Open **System Settings → General → Login Items**, click **+**, and select Butterfly from Applications.

## If Butterfly does not appear

- Open Butterfly again from Applications.
- Check the menu-bar overflow area if your screen is small.
- Click the 🦋 icon and make sure Butterfly is not paused.
- For more help, open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/gajeradhrumil38/lil-butterfly-mac/issues).
